# Customer-shopping-behavior-analysis-project
A data analysis project of Customer shopping behavior analaysis using python, SQL and powerBI.

About This Project

I built this project to analyze customer shopping behavior and understand what influences purchasing decisions in a retail business. The goal was to take raw transaction data, clean and prepare it, analyze customer trends using SQL, and create a Power BI dashboard that helps stakeholders make better business decisions.

The project follows a complete data analytics workflow, from data cleaning and feature engineering to business analysis and dashboard development.

Business Problem

A retail company wants to better understand its customers and identify opportunities to improve sales, customer engagement, and customer loyalty.

Some of the questions I wanted to answer were:

Which customer groups generate the most revenue?
Do discounts actually influence purchasing behavior?
Are subscribers spending more than non-subscribers?
Which products receive the best customer ratings?
What customer segments should the company focus on?

By answering these questions, the business can make more informed decisions about marketing campaigns, product strategy, and customer retention efforts.

Dataset

The dataset contains 3,900 customer purchase records and includes information about:

Customer demographics (age, gender, location)
Purchase details (product, category, purchase amount)
Subscription status
Review ratings
Discounts and promotions
Shipping preferences
Purchase frequency

The only missing values were found in the review rating column, which I handled during the data cleaning phase.

Tools Used
Python (Pandas, NumPy)
PostgreSQL
SQL
Power BI
Jupyter Notebook
What I Did
Data Cleaning and Preparation

Using Python and Pandas, I:

Loaded and explored the dataset
Checked for missing values
Filled missing review ratings using category-based median values
Standardized column names
Removed redundant columns
Created new features such as age groups and purchase frequency indicators

Once the data was cleaned, I loaded it into PostgreSQL for further analysis.

SQL Analysis

After preparing the data, I wrote SQL queries to answer business-related questions.

Some of the analyses included:

Revenue generated by gender
Spending patterns of discount users
Top-rated products
Shipping type comparison
Subscriber vs non-subscriber behavior
Customer segmentation
Revenue contribution by age group
Most purchased products in each category

The goal was not just to write queries but to generate insights that could help the business make better decisions.

Power BI Dashboard

I created an interactive Power BI dashboard to make the findings easier to explore and understand.

The dashboard includes:

Total customers
Average purchase amount
Average review rating
Revenue by category
Sales by category
Revenue by age group
Subscription analysis
Customer filters for deeper exploration

The dashboard allows stakeholders to quickly identify trends and compare customer segments.

Key Findings
Customer Loyalty Matters

Most customers fall into the Loyal segment, which shows that repeat purchasing behavior plays a significant role in overall business performance.

Young Adults Generated the Highest Revenue

Among all age groups, Young Adults contributed the highest total revenue, making them an important target audience for future marketing campaigns.

Express Shipping Customers Spend More

Customers who selected Express Shipping had a slightly higher average purchase amount than those using Standard Shipping.

Product Ratings Can Drive Marketing Decisions

Products such as Gloves, Sandals, and Boots received the highest average ratings and could be highlighted in promotional campaigns.

Recommendations

Based on the analysis, I would recommend:

Expanding loyalty and rewards programs for repeat customers
Promoting subscription benefits more aggressively
Highlighting top-rated products in marketing campaigns
Creating targeted campaigns for high-revenue customer groups
Reviewing discount strategies to maximize profitability while maintaining sales volume
